---
name: Frame image validation
description: Visual and licensing checks for images used in the eyeglass frame catalog.
---

When adding or remapping frame products, visually confirm each image matches its listed style and color, store it locally, and preserve its source and license. A successful URL check alone can miss irrelevant or mislabeled product photos.

**Why:** The original frame seed included external links that were broken or depicted unrelated subjects, and some remaining glasses photos did not match their catalog labels.

**How to apply:** Before adding an `imageUrl` to a frame entry, inspect the downloaded image and check it against the product metadata; keep licensing details alongside the gallery assets.
